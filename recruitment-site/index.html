Frequently asked questions

Is this a job with Handshake or Outlier?

No. We're an independent coaching team. You sign up and contract directly with the platform, and they pay you directly.

How much can I really earn?

It depends on the project, your skills and how many tasks are available. Our active taskers often earn $500 or more per week, but earnings vary and some weeks have fewer tasks.

How long until I get tasks?

Most of the people we coach get their first tasks within about 2 weeks of onboarding, but project matching is decided by the platform and is not guaranteed.

What do I need?

A laptop or PC, a stable internet connection, a valid ID, a bank or payout account in your name, and good attention to detail. A degree or specialist skill helps you get higher-paying projects.

How do you make money?

We earn official platform referral bonuses and offer optional paid coaching. You'll always know any cost upfront, before you pay anything.

Will you need access to my account?

Never. You do your own tasks on your own device. We only coach and guide you.
